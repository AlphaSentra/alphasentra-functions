Monte Carlo Simulation Paths

How to interpret this:

  • Simulated Paths: Each faint line represents one possible future trajectory of the portfolio value, generated by randomly sampling from the historical return distribution. The chart shows hundreds of potential outcomes over the selected time horizon.
  • Median Path (Bold): The central tendency — half of simulations fall below this line, half above. This is the most likely expected path given current risk/return characteristics.
  • Shaded Bands: Typically show confidence intervals (e.g., 25th–75th percentile, 5th–95th percentile). Narrow bands indicate stable, predictable outcomes; wide bands signal higher uncertainty and volatility.
  • Distribution of Endpoints: The spread of final values across all paths illustrates the range of possible portfolio outcomes (e.g., "There's a 5% chance the portfolio falls below X"). Heavily skewed paths indicate asymmetric risk (e.g., "lottery ticket" upside or crash risk).
  • What's Not Shown: These simulations assume the future will resemble the past (stationarity). They do not account for structural breaks, regime shifts, or black swan events outside historical experience.
